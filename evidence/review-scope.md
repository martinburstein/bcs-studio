# Internal review scope and limits

This public summary describes qualifications already stated in the published casebook. The private lab retains the full review packets, source captures, superseded calculations, access failures and operational receipts. Those private files are not included in this public release.

The 22 core company cases received dated primary-source review, different-author internal AI-assisted challenge, and source/unit/arithmetic checks. The ten auxiliary studies remain outside admission; none is a verified trade. These reviews share source materials and model families. They are not independent raw data, external audits, real-person endorsements or scholarly peer review.

The auxiliary cases preserve material qualifications. AEHL and AGMH have source-access or issuer-report limitations; uncollected receivables and disposal entries are not cash. ARTL's proposed SOL financing is unresolved and current account/trade readiness is not established. AGPU lacks readable executed customer-contract evidence. APLD's funding obligations, project cash availability and parent recourse require further contract-level reconciliation. AURE's reported token positions do not establish shareholder redemption rights or collected gold. The casebook supplies the dated source links, calculations, strongest opposing cases and narrower conclusions.

The source-order, transport, timestamp and preservation qualifications of the internal process remain part of the private record. Removing operational detail from this public edition does not turn a qualified review into an unqualified one. Preliminary MSTR, PYPL and CME studies that still lacked different-author challenge were excluded from this release.

No live price, listed put series, approved account permission, borrow/carry cost, funded position or stressed collection/exit has been established for a trade. Substantive corrections belong in a new private-lab review and a versioned public edition.
